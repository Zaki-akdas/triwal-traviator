BEFORE YOU WANDER

A few good
things to know.

Still wondering how it all works? Start here. If your question has a passport, we'll happily talk it through.

Are the itinerary packages fixed departures?

No. The destination journeys and package ideas are flexible starting points, not scheduled departures or confirmed reservations. A trip designer can shape the route, pace, stays and experiences around your dates and interests.

Can I use the itinerary maker without booking?

Absolutely. The studio creates a starting-point itinerary you can save or copy from this device. If you'd like help turning it into a trip, send it to a travel designer using the “Talk it through” button.

Are the prices on this site final?

No prices are quoted here. Any example pricing shown in a future proposal would be indicative until dates, availability, inclusions and the final itinerary are confirmed in writing.

Can you help with a trip that isn't listed?

Of course. The places above are a few starting points. Share the place you have in mind — or just tell us the kind of experience you're after.

Does the AI planner book flights or hotels?

No. It creates an itinerary idea only. It doesn't check live availability, make reservations or replace destination-specific advice from a travel professional.
