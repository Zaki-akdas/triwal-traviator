# India Field Guide image credits

The India Field Guide contains 57 destination profiles, and every profile now uses a locally stored, photo-style JPG: 28 AI-generated illustrations, 20 Unsplash photographs, and 9 Wikimedia Commons photographs. Images are visual guides, not proof of current site conditions or a guarantee that a landmark or view appears exactly as shown. The detail dialog labels AI-generated images as illustrations and links sourced photographs to their source pages.
